




# 1 What is derivative , gradient 

The **derivative** measures how a function changes **as its input changes**. It tells you the **slope** (rate of change) of a function at a particular point.

![[image/Pasted image 20250528100642.png]]



**Gradient** (in multivariable calculus):
The **gradient** is a generalization of the derivative for functions with **multiple inputs**. It is a **vector** of all partial derivatives of a function.

![[image/Pasted image 20250528100616.png]]


|Term|Applies To|Output Type|Meaning|
|---|---|---|---|
|Derivative|Single-variable function|Scalar|Slope / rate of change at a point|
|Gradient|Multi-variable function|Vector|Direction & rate of steepest increase|
